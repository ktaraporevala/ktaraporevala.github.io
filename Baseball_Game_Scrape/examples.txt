Base codes involving batter
#...         (forms of outs)
 C/E%         (interference)
 S$           (single, $ is optional)
 D$           (double)
 T$           (triple)
 DGR          (ground rule double)
E$ or $E$    (error allowing batter to get on base)
 FC$          (fielder's choice, batter to first)
 FO$          (force out, seems like above, batter to first)
 FLE$         (error on foul fly ball, can be ignored)
 H or HR      (home run leaving the park)
 H$ or HR$    (inside the park home run)
 HP           (hit by pitch, batter to first)
 K            (strikeout)
K+event      (strikeout and more, can be SB%, CS%, OA, PO%, PB, WP and E$)
 NP           (no play, can be ignored)
 I or IW      (intentional walk, batter to first)
 W            (walk, batter to first)
W+event      (walk and more, can be SB%, CS%, PO%, PB, WP and E$)

Base codes not involving batter (batter still player_id)
BK           (balk)
 CS%($$)      (caught stealing, % is base runner was tryna get to)
DI           (defensive indifference, steal basically but should be treated different?)
OA           (other runner advance not covered by codes, may be out)
 PB           (passed ball)
 WP           (wild pitch)
 PO%($$)      (picked off of base, % is base of runner picked off)
PO%(E$)      (attempted pickoff error)
 POCS%($$)    (pickoff caught stealing, % is base runner was tryna)
 SB%          (stolen base, % is base tryna)


Advancement codes   (starts with .)
#-#        (first # is initial base, second # is final base)
#-#;#-#    (can be multiple)
B-#        (batter advancement, so far looks like can be ignored if to first)
